# Hi, I’m Octavio López, a multidisciplinary designer 
# working across brand, digital, and web design.

I have several years of professional experience in branding, visual communication, editorial design, and digital projects. Throughout my career, I’ve developed visual identities, campaigns, publications, digital experiences, presentations, and multimedia content for international teams and clients across different industries.

More recently, I’ve been expanding my skills into UX/UI and web development, combining my design background with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and other web technologies. I’m interested in understanding the complete process behind digital products—from defining the visual direction and user experience to actually building and implementing the final interface.

My transition into technology came from wanting to understand what happens beyond the design file. After years of designing digital experiences and working closely with technical teams, I became increasingly interested in how interfaces are structured, developed, and brought to life through code. This led me to study Full Stack Web Development at TripleTen and start building my own web projects.

I also have experience managing projects from initial client conversations and requirements through execution and final delivery. I’ve worked with international and U.S. teams, coordinating feedback, timelines, deliverables, and communication across different departments.

## Professional Goal

I’m looking for opportunities as a Web Designer, Digital Designer, UI/UX Designer, or Frontend Developer where I can combine my design experience with my growing technical skills.

I’m especially interested in projects where design and technology overlap, allowing me to contribute to both the visual and functional aspects of a digital product.
